Algerian round, soft flatbread.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup semolina
- 1 cup flour
- 1 tbs oil
- 1 tsp yeast
- salt
- water

## Instructions

1. Add all together and mix.
2. Knead for a long time.
3. Let it rest.
4. Knead again.
5. Shape and let it rest on a towel.
6. Dust with flour to ensure it does not stick.
7. Bake.

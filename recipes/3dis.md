Mami's 3dess recipe. Perfect for the cold Scottish weather.

## Ingredients

- 3dess
- onion
- garlic
- tin tomatoes
- pepper paste
- salt
- black pepper
- cumin
- hot water

## Instructions

1. Fey onion, garlic, salt, black pepper and cumin.
2. Separate wash the 3dis and leave in hot water.
3. Add tin tomatoes, pepper paste, water and 3dis to pot.
4. Let cook.
